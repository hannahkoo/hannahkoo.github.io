About This System

This suite was developed internally by me, as the static lead, to streamline banner localization across 78 languages. It grew out of hands-on production experience — every feature exists because a real bottleneck needed solving. And it's never stood still: the toolkit is maintained and evolves to fit the needs of each new project and client.

Note — the interface shown in the reel isn't the actual tool UI. It's an abstract version created for the highlight reel.

Same budget. 40% more output.

Leadership set a 35–40% surge in volume. Under the old workflow, absorbing it would have meant scaling the team. But the bottleneck wasn't hours — it was manual repetition. So instead of adding headcount, I automated the friction.

  • 83% faster production — a ~25-hour manual lift compressed to a ~2-hour focused pass
  • +3.0 FTE of capacity added — more throughput, no new headcount
  • $3.1k /yr saved by replacing paid third-party plugin licenses

On a standard 30-language, 270-banner build, that's roughly 18–23 hours reclaimed per major project — redirected out of mechanical setup and into design judgment and client-facing iteration.

A few focused tools, not one big script.

The suite is modular by design — each tool removes one specific source of manual rework, and together they cover the full localized-banner pipeline from setup to export.

Client-safe by design — pure JavaScript, zero dependencies, no external CDNs or third-party libraries. Client IP is removed from working files automatically.

Designed around how the team actually works.

None of this started as a spec. Each tool was built, watched in real use, and reshaped against how people actually moved through a build — where they hesitated, what they re-did by hand, which steps quietly broke at volume. The engineering was the easy part; knowing which friction was worth removing came from doing the production work myself.

An institutional asset — and compounding.

  • Documented & standardized. Templates and workflow are codified, so the efficiency gains transfer to every designer on the floor — not just me.
  • Owned, not rented. In-house tool replaces third-party SaaS. The advantage doesn't walk out the door, and it recurs every year.
  • Built to scale. Higher volume without higher overhead — the system absorbs the surge the headcount can't.
